## Method

- **Word count** is the article body only (after front matter), counted by whitespace split.
- **Drinks linked** is the distinct set of `/caffeine-in/` slugs a post references: its `drinks:` front matter (added in Phase 2.2) plus any slug already linked inline in the body that wasn't in that list (a few posts, e.g. `caffeine-pills-supplements`, had inline drink links before Phase 2 ran).
- **Clusters** start from the six named groups and five named pairs in the plan (Sleep & timing, Mitochondria, Stress & hormones, Heart & vessels, Metabolism, Hydration, lymphatic/migraine/gut/menstrual/bone), plus seven more overlapping clusters found by actually reading titles and topics across all 76 posts: **Genetics & epigenetics**, **Brain & cognition**, **Immune & inflammation**, **Exercise & performance**, **Tolerance & withdrawal**, **Nutrient intake**, and **Anxiety** (the last three pairs/clusters are noted but not merged — see below). Posts with no real overlap keep a `Standalone — <topic>` label and default to Keep.
- **Merge targets** were picked by comparing word count, reference-list size (links under each post's "References" section, a proxy for how well-sourced it is), and title/search-intent clarity within each cluster — not by title alone. Two posts sharing a title pattern (e.g. "X: Risks and Safe Limits") but covering different organs/conditions are *not* treated as overlapping; that pattern repeats across ~7 standalone posts (liver, thyroid, teens, hair loss, acne, skin, kidney stones) and each targets a distinct, non-competing query.

## Judgment calls worth the owner's attention

- **Stress & hormones (5 posts)** is the largest merge group: cortisol, general stress-response, adrenal function, hormones, and endocrine-system posts cover very similar HPA-axis ground. Proposed target is `caffeine-hormones-response` (broadest natural umbrella, solid refs/word count), but `caffeine-cortisol-stress` has the clearest single-keyword search intent ("caffeine and cortisol") and the most drinks linked (5) — worth checking GSC before committing to a target.
- **Heart & vessels**: proposed target `caffeine-blood-pressure-risks-safe-limits` (longest, most drinks linked, and already a plan-protected "risks/safe limits" Keep-pattern topic) absorbs three mechanism-focused posts (vessel function, cardiac health, nitric oxide) that largely explain *why* blood pressure changes.
- **Metabolism**: only `caffeine-thermogenesis-weight` (907 words, the shortest post in this cluster) is proposed to merge into `caffeine-fat-metabolism`. `caffeine-glycogen-levels` is left as Keep despite being in the plan's named cluster — it's really an exercise/glycogen-depletion topic (see Exercise & performance below), not a weight-loss/diet topic, so merging it into `fat-metabolism` would mismatch reader intent.
- **Genetics & epigenetics**: `caffeine-genetics-effects` and `caffeine-epigenetics-gene-expression` are kept separate (distinct enough mechanisms: inherited CYP1A2 variation vs. gene-expression changes). `caffeine-methylation-homocysteine` is flagged **Noindex/remove candidate** — a technical one-carbon-cycle biochemistry topic with the weakest practical tie to caffeine safety of any post audited.
- **Brain & cognition**: `caffeine-brain-neuroscience-basics` is the clear pillar (broadest query, foundational). `caffeine-cognitive-flexibility` and `caffeine-silent-brain-areas` are narrower academic framings proposed to merge into it. `caffeine-memory-consolidation` is kept separate — it sits at the sleep/memory intersection with the most drinks linked (4) in this cluster and ties directly into sleep-safety advice.
- **Immune & inflammation**: `caffeine-oxidative-stress` is proposed to merge into `caffeine-inflammatory-response` (tightly linked biology, both well-sourced); `caffeine-immune-system` is kept separate as a distinct, broader query.
- **Exercise & performance**, **Tolerance & withdrawal**, **Nutrient intake**, **Anxiety**: noted as related pairs/groups but only one merge among them — `caffeine-tolerance-reset-guide` (827 words, fewest references of any post) into `caffeine-addictive-withdrawal-symptoms`, and `caffeine-nutrient-intake` into `caffeine-nutrition-optimization`. The exercise pair (performance vs. recovery) and anxiety pair (general vs. teens — teens is explicitly plan-protected) target different enough queries to keep separate.
- **Noindex/remove candidates (3, kept deliberately short per the plan's YMYL caution):** `caffeine-methylation-homocysteine` (above), `caffeine-hair-loss-safe-limits` (the post itself states "no strong evidence" oral caffeine causes hair loss), and `caffeine-hearing-risks` (its own intro calls the caffeine/hearing link "one of medicine's more confusing puzzles," evidence is mixed/animal-only). Two other borderline topics — `caffeine-nerve-sensitivity` and `caffeine-libido-connection` — were considered but kept as Keep: both tie caffeine dose/timing to a genuine physiological mechanism rather than resting on a debunked claim.
- `caffeine-half-life-sleep` has `redirect_to: /half-life/` in its front matter and renders as a pure redirect stub (confirmed in Phase 1/2 builds) — it has no real body content to audit, so it's excluded from cluster/action logic entirely rather than forced into Keep or Merge.
