## A little more, thanks to the chocolate

The Mocha Frappuccino carries 70 mg of caffeine in a Tall, 100 mg in a Grande, and 130 mg in a Venti, a touch above the plain [Coffee Frappuccino]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-coffee-frappuccino/' | relative_url }}) (65, 95 and 125 mg) and the [Caramel Frappuccino]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caramel-frappuccino/' | relative_url }}) (60, 90 and 120 mg) at the same sizes. The pattern mirrors Starbucks' hot drinks, where a [Caffè Mocha]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caffe-mocha/' | relative_url }}) also runs higher than a same-size latte, because the mocha sauce itself carries a little caffeine on top of the coffee base.

## Still far below an espresso drink

Despite the extra mocha caffeine, a Grande Mocha Frappuccino's 100 mg remains well under a Grande [Caffè Latte]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caffe-latte/' | relative_url }}) (150 mg), since Frappuccinos are blended with a coffee base rather than pulled as espresso shots.

## Blended coffee, not brewed coffee

A Frappuccino's coffee base is a pre-made concentrate blended with ice, milk and syrup, rather than freshly brewed coffee poured over ice, which is part of why its caffeine per ounce runs lower than a straightforward iced coffee or cold brew of the same size. The mocha flavouring narrows that gap slightly compared to the plain Coffee Frappuccino, but doesn't close it.

## Where it fits in a day

At 100 mg, a Grande is well under the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults. Four Grandes, 400 mg, would reach the daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, two Grandes use the whole day's allowance.
