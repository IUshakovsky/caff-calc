## Same figure as a latte, foam aside

USDA's survey data gives cappuccino the identical caffeine density as a latte, 36 mg per 100 g, because both drinks are built from the same ratio of espresso to milk; a cappuccino just uses more of that milk as foam instead of liquid. That works out to 86 mg in an 8 fl oz cup, 130 mg in a 12 fl oz Small, 173 mg in a 16 fl oz Medium and 216 mg in a 20 fl oz Large. The foam changes mouthfeel and how quickly you drink it, not the caffeine underneath.

A coffee-shop cappuccino follows a different rule, because chains size caffeine by shot count rather than cup volume. Starbucks lists a Grande Cappuccino at 150 mg, the same two-shot dose as its [Grande Caffè Latte]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caffe-latte/' | relative_url }}), so the chain version and the 16 oz USDA figure (173 mg) land close together even though the shot-based drink doesn't grow with size the way the survey figure does.

## Against straight espresso

A cappuccino's caffeine ultimately comes from its espresso shots. A single [espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/espresso/' | relative_url }}) shot carries 63 mg by USDA's figure, so an 8 oz cappuccino's 86 mg is roughly what one strong shot or a shot-and-a-bit contributes once milk is added. A double shot (126 mg) is closer to what a 12 oz Small cappuccino delivers.

## How the sizes stack up against a daily limit

A Small at 130 mg leaves more than 270 mg of the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. The Large size, 216 mg, is the one that crosses the 200 mg [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as a safe single dose for healthy adults, by 16 mg, so a Large is worth counting as a full dose on its own rather than pairing it with much else in the same sitting.

Two Small cappuccinos in a day, 260 mg, still leave room under the FDA figure; add a third and you're at 390 mg, close enough to the ceiling that anything else caffeinated that day should be small.
