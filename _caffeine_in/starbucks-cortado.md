## Small cup, big dose

Starbucks' Cortado comes in one size only: an 8 oz cup with three ristretto shots of blonde espresso and a little steamed milk, 230 mg of caffeine. That is more than a Grande [Flat White]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-flat-white/' | relative_url }}) (195 mg) in half the volume, and about three times an 8 oz cup of [drip coffee]({{ '/caffeine-in/brewed-coffee/' | relative_url }}) at USDA's reference strength (95 mg).

Ristretto shots are pulled shorter than a standard shot, which concentrates the espresso rather than diluting it, and three of them in a small cup is why the cortado punches well above its size.

## The strongest small drink on the menu

Among Starbucks' short-format espresso drinks, the Cortado's 230 mg beats a Doppio [Starbucks Espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-espresso/' | relative_url }}) (two shots, 150 mg) and even a Triple (225 mg), despite fitting in the same 8 oz cup as a Short latte or cappuccino at 75 mg.

The single-size format also means there's no way to order a smaller cortado to reduce the dose the way you could ask for a Short cappuccino; asking for fewer shots is the only way to bring the caffeine down while keeping the drink's style.

## Where it fits in a day

At 230 mg, a single Cortado is over the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults, on its own. It is a little over half the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, a single Cortado already exceeds the whole day's allowance.
