## 200 mg in a soda-sized can

Alani Nu Energy is sold in 12 oz cans that look and drink like a soda but contain 200 mg of caffeine. That is the same caffeine as two small cups of brewed coffee in a volume that can be consumed in a few minutes. The small volume makes it easy to drink quickly, so the caffeine hits faster than it would from a larger, slower-to-drink coffee.

The manufacturer lists 200 mg per can across its flavours. Because the caffeine is added rather than brewed, the dose is consistent from can to can. A single can is already half of the 400 mg daily reference the FDA cites for healthy adults and matches the 200 mg ceiling ACOG recommends during pregnancy.

## Comparing to other energy drinks

At 200 mg in 12 oz, Alani Nu is stronger per ounce than [Red Bull]({{ '/caffeine-in/red-bull/' | relative_url }}) (80 mg in 8.4 oz) and close to [Celsius Original]({{ '/caffeine-in/celsius-original/' | relative_url }}) (200 mg in 12 oz). It is lower in caffeine than [Celsius Essentials]({{ '/caffeine-in/celsius-essentials/' | relative_url }}) (270 mg in 16 oz) but higher than a 16 oz [Monster Energy]({{ '/caffeine-in/monster-energy-original/' | relative_url }}) (160 mg), and the smaller can makes it the more concentrated dose of the two.

If you are using energy drinks before exercise, 200 mg is within the 3–6 mg/kg range for many adults. If you are using it as an afternoon pick-me-up, the caffeine can still be present at bedtime because caffeine's half-life averages about five hours with a wide individual range.
