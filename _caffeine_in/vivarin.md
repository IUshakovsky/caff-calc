## The same 200 mg as its category

Vivarin carries 200 mg of caffeine per tablet, by its drug label. That matches [NoDoz Maximum Strength]({{ '/caffeine-in/nodoz-maximum-strength/' | relative_url }}) and a generic [caffeine tablet]({{ '/caffeine-in/caffeine-pill/' | relative_url }}) exactly, so the choice between them is packaging and price rather than dose. One tablet carries roughly the caffeine of two and a half standard 8.4 oz cans of Red Bull, or about two 8 oz cups of [drip coffee]({{ '/caffeine-in/brewed-coffee/' | relative_url }}) at USDA's reference strength (95 mg each), delivered in one swallow instead of a cup you drink over several minutes.

At 200 mg, a single Vivarin tablet sits right at the 200 mg [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as a safe single dose for healthy adults, and two tablets reach the full 400 mg the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites as the daily figure for healthy adults, with nothing else counted.

## Worth checking against other medicine

Caffeine tablets are easy to overlook as a medicine interaction because they're sold near cold and allergy remedies rather than with obvious stimulants. Some pain relievers already include caffeine as an active ingredient: [Excedrin Extra Strength]({{ '/caffeine-in/excedrin-extra-strength/' | relative_url }}) carries 65 mg per caplet, 130 mg at its normal two-caplet dose, and taking Vivarin alongside a dose of Excedrin adds up to 330 mg before any coffee or soda. The Vivarin label directs limiting other caffeine-containing medicines, foods and drinks while using it for exactly this reason, and advises asking a doctor or pharmacist first if you're taking other drugs.

## Dosing and limits

The label allows one tablet every 3 to 4 hours as needed, not to exceed the directions on the package, and says not to use it in children under 12 or as a substitute for sleep. The FDA's warning about concentrated caffeine sources is a useful anchor here too: it estimates toxic effects can appear around 1,200 mg taken rapidly, six tablets' worth, a dose that a day of stacking Vivarin against a headache and a poor night's sleep could approach faster than it looks.
