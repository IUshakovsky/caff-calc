## A concentrated shot, not a sip of coffee

5-hour Energy Regular Strength is sold in a 1.93 oz bottle and contains 200 mg of caffeine. The small volume is the main safety issue: it can be consumed in seconds, delivers two small cups' worth of caffeine, and gives none of the slow-drinking cues that a 12 oz coffee provides. The brand also makes an Extra Strength version with a higher dose, but this page refers to Regular Strength.

A single Regular Strength shot is half of the 400 mg daily reference the FDA cites for healthy adults. It is also equal to the 200 mg ceiling ACOG recommends during pregnancy, which means one shot uses the entire daily allowance for that group. If you take a shot in the afternoon, the caffeine is likely still in your system at bedtime because the average half-life is about five hours with a wide individual range.

## Why shots lead to accidental overconsumption

Energy shots are easy to underestimate because the bottle is tiny. Unlike coffee, where you can gauge progress by the cup, a shot is gone in one gulp. Taking two Regular Strength shots reaches 400 mg by itself. Combining one shot with a coffee, tea or soda can push you over the daily ceiling without the volume making the total obvious.

The 200 mg per 1.93 oz is the highest caffeine concentration of any drink on the site. Use it only when you have already counted the rest of your day.
