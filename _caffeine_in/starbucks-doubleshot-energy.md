## Coffee-based, not a typical energy drink

Starbucks Doubleshot Energy, Coffee is built around real espresso rather than the guarana or synthetic-caffeine blends most energy drinks use. A 15 fl oz can carries 135 mg of caffeine, roughly the same as two Solo shots of [Starbucks Espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-espresso/' | relative_url }}) (150 mg), which matches the "doubleshot" in the name.

## Weaker than the big energy-drink names

Despite the "energy" branding, this can carries less caffeine than a similarly sized [Monster Energy Original]({{ '/caffeine-in/monster-energy-original/' | relative_url }}), which packs 160 mg into 16 oz. It has less caffeine per ounce than a standard [Red Bull]({{ '/caffeine-in/red-bull/' | relative_url }}) too: a 12 oz Red Bull carries 114 mg in a smaller can. It is nowhere near an [energy shot]({{ '/caffeine-in/5-hour-energy-regular/' | relative_url }}) like 5-hour Energy, which packs 200 mg into under 2 oz.

## A coffee-flavoured energy drink

Because it's marketed alongside energy drinks but built from actual espresso, Doubleshot Energy sits in a middle category: real coffee flavour and a coffee-scale caffeine dose, packaged and sold like an energy drink for convenience. That distinguishes it from drinks that use guarana extract or synthetic caffeine to hit a similar number without any coffee in the ingredient list at all.

## Where it fits in a day

At 135 mg, a can sits well under the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults. Two cans, 270 mg, still leave room under the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, one can uses just over half the day's allowance; the FDA also notes that medical experts advise against energy drinks for children and teens.
