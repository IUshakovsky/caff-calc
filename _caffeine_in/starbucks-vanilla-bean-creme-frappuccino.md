## No coffee, no caffeine

Unlike the [Coffee Frappuccino]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-coffee-frappuccino/' | relative_url }}) and its flavoured relatives, the Vanilla Bean Crème Frappuccino has no coffee base at all, just milk, vanilla bean powder and ice. Starbucks lists 0 mg of caffeine at every size, Tall through Venti, making it a genuine zero, on par with a plain [herbal tea]({{ '/caffeine-in/herbal-tea/' | relative_url }}).

## "Crème" is the tell, not a guarantee

The "Crème" naming is Starbucks' signal for a Frappuccino built without coffee, and it's a useful cue when ordering for a child or anyone avoiding caffeine entirely. But it isn't an absolute rule across every flavour Starbucks has offered under that label over time, so checking the specific drink's published nutrition, or asking whether it contains coffee or espresso, is worth doing before assuming zero.

## A base for other caffeine-free swaps

Because this Frappuccino has no coffee base, it's also the starting point Starbucks baristas sometimes use to make other flavours caffeine-free on request, substituting the Vanilla Bean base for a coffee-based one while keeping a similar syrup and topping. The result approximates the flavour of a coffee Frappuccino without any of its caffeine, though it isn't identical.

## Where it fits in a day

At 0 mg, this drink adds nothing to any daily total, including the 400 mg the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults or the 200 mg daily ceiling [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses in pregnancy. It's one of the few Starbucks drinks that's a safe pick for anyone cutting caffeine out entirely, or for children, without needing to do any math.
