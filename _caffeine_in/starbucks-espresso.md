## The building block of the whole menu

Ordered on its own, Starbucks espresso comes as a Solo (one shot, 75 mg), Doppio (two shots, 150 mg, the default order), Triple (three shots, 225 mg) or Quad (four shots, 300 mg). Each shot adds a fixed 75 mg, so the drink scales in a straight line: a Quad is exactly four times a Solo. That 75 mg per shot figure, a little above USDA's generic [espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/espresso/' | relative_url }}) figure of about 63 mg for a 1 oz shot, is the number behind most of Starbucks' hot menu: a Grande [Caffè Latte]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caffe-latte/' | relative_url }}) is two shots (150 mg), and an Americano adds a shot at every size step.

## Blonde shots run higher

Signature (dark) espresso shots are 75 mg each; blonde espresso, Starbucks' lighter-roast shot used in drinks like the [Blonde Vanilla Latte]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-blonde-vanilla-latte/' | relative_url }}), carries 85 mg a shot instead. Asking for a Doppio "with blonde" swaps 150 mg for 170 mg, without changing the drink's size or the number of shots.

Ordering straight espresso rather than a milk drink also means nothing dilutes the dose, so the shot count on the receipt is the whole story, unlike a latte or cappuccino where flavoring and milk volume can make the strength harder to judge by taste alone.

## Where it fits in a day

A Doppio, 150 mg, sits under the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults; a Triple (225 mg) is over it. A Quad, 300 mg, is three-quarters of the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults, on its own. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, even a Doppio uses most of the day's allowance.
