## The shot count, not the cup, sets the caffeine

A latte is espresso and steamed milk, and Starbucks pulls a fixed number of shots per size. Each shot of its signature [espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/espresso/' | relative_url }}) carries 75 mg. Short and Tall lattes get one shot (75 mg); Grande and hot Venti get two (150 mg). So a hot Venti, 20 oz, has exactly the caffeine of a Grande, and a Tall exactly that of a Short. The bigger hot size buys more milk, not more caffeine.

The iced latte breaks the pattern. The iced Venti is a 24 oz cup with three shots, 225 mg, one more than the hot Venti.

## Much less than brewed coffee

A Grande latte has less than half the caffeine of a Grande [Pike Place Roast]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-pike-place-roast/' | relative_url }}) (315 to 390 mg), and less than a Grande [Cold Brew]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-cold-brew/' | relative_url }}) (205 mg). Against home coffee it lands between one and two 8 oz cups of [drip coffee]({{ '/caffeine-in/brewed-coffee/' | relative_url }}) at USDA's reference strength (95 mg each).

The flavoured lattes follow the same shot structure: a Grande Pumpkin Spice Latte, White Chocolate Mocha or Caramel Macchiato carries the same 150 mg. The exceptions use different espresso. A Grande Blonde Vanilla Latte has 170 mg, because blonde shots carry 85 mg each, and a Grande Flat White, made with ristretto shots, has 195 mg.

## Adding it to your day

Two Grande lattes, 300 mg, leave 100 mg of the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. At 150 mg, a Grande is also under the 200 mg that [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as a safe single dose for healthy adults.

In pregnancy, working to the 200 mg daily ceiling [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses, one Grande latte leaves 50 mg for the rest of the day: about a Grande Starbucks hot chocolate (25 mg) and an ounce of [dark chocolate]({{ '/caffeine-in/dark-chocolate-70-85/' | relative_url }}) (23 mg). A Tall latte, at 75 mg, leaves far more room.
