## A little less than plain, because of the cream

A Grande Vanilla Sweet Cream Cold Brew carries 185 mg of caffeine: 145 mg in a Tall, 185 mg in a Grande, 275 mg in a Venti, and 315 mg in a Trenta. That is slightly below plain [Starbucks Cold Brew]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-cold-brew/' | relative_url }}) at the same sizes (155, 205, 310 and 360 mg), because a float of vanilla sweet cream takes up room in the cup that would otherwise be more cold-brew concentrate.

It is well below [Nitro Cold Brew]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-nitro-cold-brew/' | relative_url }}), which is not cut with cream or much ice and carries 280 mg in a Grande, the most concentrated cold coffee on the menu.

## Same base as the seasonal Pumpkin Cream version

Starbucks' [Pumpkin Cream Cold Brew]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-pumpkin-cream-cold-brew/' | relative_url }}) uses the identical cold-brew base and matches this drink's caffeine exactly at every size; only the topping (pumpkin cream instead of vanilla sweet cream) differs.

Asking for light cream, or no cream at all, brings the drink closer to plain Cold Brew's higher figures, since less of the cup is then taken up by the sweet cream float.

## Where it fits in a day

At 185 mg, a Grande sits under the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults; the 275 mg Venti is over it. Two Grandes, 370 mg, come close to the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites for healthy adults. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, even a Tall is close to the whole day's allowance.
