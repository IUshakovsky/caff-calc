## One more shot at Venti size

Iced and hot [Caffè Lattes]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-caffe-latte/' | relative_url }}) match at the smaller sizes: 75 mg in a Tall, 150 mg in a Grande, both built from one and two shots of espresso. The Venti is where they split. The hot Venti is a 20 oz cup with two shots, 150 mg, the same as a Grande. The iced Venti is a bigger 24 oz cup with three shots, 225 mg, because Starbucks adds a shot rather than just more ice and milk to fill the larger cold cup.

## Against other iced options

A Grande iced latte's 150 mg is well below a Grande [Iced Shaken Espresso]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-iced-shaken-espresso/' | relative_url }}) (225 mg), which uses more shots and less milk, and below a Grande [Cold Brew]({{ '/caffeine-in/starbucks-cold-brew/' | relative_url }}) (205 mg), which is steeped rather than pulled as espresso. It is close to an 8 oz cup of [drip coffee]({{ '/caffeine-in/brewed-coffee/' | relative_url }}) at USDA's reference strength (95 mg) and a half.

Ordering the iced Venti for the extra shot, rather than just more ice, is worth knowing if you're trying to match a hot-size habit: asking for a hot Venti instead keeps the caffeine at 150 mg, the same as a Grande.

## Where it fits in a day

At 150 mg, a Grande iced latte sits under the 200 mg single dose [EFSA](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/4102) assessed as safe for healthy adults; the 225 mg Venti is over it. Two Grandes, 300 mg, leave 100 mg of the 400 mg daily figure the [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/spilling-beans-how-much-caffeine-too-much) cites. In pregnancy, where [ACOG](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/committee-opinion/articles/2010/08/moderate-caffeine-consumption-during-pregnancy) uses a 200 mg daily ceiling, even a Tall uses over a third of the day's allowance.
